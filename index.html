All I Know About Drumming

Drumming patterns

The money beat

  • Snare Drum on 2 and 4
  • Drumm Kick on 1 and 3
  • Hihat on 1, 2, 3, 4

This beat creates a simple but catchy 4/4 beat that is easy to play. This beat is also in many songs such as:

  1. Billie Jean
  2. Back in Black
  3. Beat It
  4. Highway to Hell
  5. Another One Bites the Dust

The swing beat (jazz)

  • Closed Hihat on 1, 3, and 4 with playing 3 and 4 faster than 1
  • Foot pedal on 4
  • You can experiment with the snare drum but play the snare drum softly or you can hit the rim instead

This creates a nice sound that you can then turn into whiplash or do a drum fill while playing in a band or at home. This beat is a little tricky to master but when you do you can add ghost notes.
