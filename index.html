Drumming patterns

The money beat

  • Snare Drum on 2 and 4
  • Drumm Kick on 1 and 3
  • Hihat on 1, 2, 3, 4

This beat creates simple but catchy 4/4 beat that is easy to play. This beat is also in many songs such as.

    Bilie jean Back in black Beat it Highway to hell another one Bites the dust

The swing beat (jass)

  • Closed Hihat on 1, 3, and 4 with playing 3 and 4 faster then 1
  • Foot pedal on 4
  • you can experiment with the snare drum but play the snare drum softly or you can hit the rim instead

    • This creates a nice sound that you can then turn into whiplash or do a drum fill while playing in a n=band or at home. This beat is a little tricky to master but when you do you can add ghost notes
